# 50-patch — L1-s3 (Emotion Badge)

## slide-13 (support tier)
- severity: high
- type: differentiation
- lanes: [codex, opencode, hermes]
- problem: Extend offers only stretch tasks (A+B icons, custom image, shake) with no support tier for learners who need a scaffold to reach the core two-button badge (R0 `missing: differentiation`).
- fix: Add a paired support route — pre-built remix project, step-by-step tutorial, or guided one-button build — alongside the existing stretch challenges.
- cites: ["https://microbit.org/teach/lessons/step-counter/", "MID:principle-low-floor-high-ceiling"]

## slide-12 (evaluate success criteria)
- severity: high
- type: assessment
- lanes: [codex, opencode, hermes]
- problem: Evaluate prompts are broad and subjective with no observable success criterion or recorded evidence that a learner can explain the button-event input/output relationship (R0 `missing: assessment`).
- fix: Insert a short teacher-observed demo/exit-ticket: learner presses a button, shows the resulting icon, and states which event block caused it.
- cites: ["https://mbit.io/tutorial-emotion-badge", "https://microbit.org/teach/lessons/"]

## slide-13 (accessible input)
- severity: medium
- type: differentiation
- lanes: [codex, opencode, hermes]
- problem: All suggested extensions assume reliable use of physical buttons or shake gesture, with no alternative input for learners with fine-motor or mobility needs.
- fix: Offer an accessible substitute trigger (touch-logo, sound, or external switch) for the core/extension tasks where hardware and learner need permit it.
- cites: ["https://microbit.org/accessibility/fine-motor-and-mobility/"]

## slide-14 (share as demo)
- severity: medium
- type: assessment
- lanes: [codex, opencode, hermes]
- problem: Closing Share is teacher-led Q&A restating objectives rather than a peer demo/physical show, so misconceptions can hide behind whole-class recall and the relatedness/competence element (Self-Determination Theory) is missed.
- fix: Convert Share into paired peer-share with a sentence stem ("My micro:bit shows ___ when I press ___") and a physical demonstration of the badge, sampled by the teacher against stated objectives.
- cites: ["https://microbit.org/projects/make-it-code-it/beating-heart/", "MID:self-determination-theory", "MID:formative-assessment-lightweight-kids"]

## slide-7 (predict before reveal)
- severity: medium
- type: pedagogy
- lanes: [codex, opencode, hermes]
- problem: The "examine the code" step reveals the completed program with no prediction prompt, missing a check of the input-to-output mental model before learners copy code.
- fix: Add a short prediction question ("What will the LEDs show when I press button A?") before revealing/running the completed program.
- cites: ["MID:cognitive-load-theory", "https://mbit.io/tutorial-emotion-badge"]

## slide-4 (concrete anchor for input/output)
- severity: medium
- type: pedagogy
- lanes: [hermes]
- problem: Input/output learning objectives state abstract definitions ("data sent to/from a computer") with no concrete hands-on anchor for ages 7-9.
- fix: Tie the definitions to a tangible physical example (pressing a real button, seeing a real light) before the abstract wording.
- cites: ["MID:concrete-physical-examples", "MID:first-principles"]
