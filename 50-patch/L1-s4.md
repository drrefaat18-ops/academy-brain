## slide-6 (base vs Extend display conflated)
- severity: medium
- type: technical
- lanes: [opencode, hermes]
- problem: Slide 6 narrates the count displaying immediately after every shake, but the canonical/Extend structure shows count on button A press — the deck conflates base and Extend behaviour without signposting.
- fix: State explicitly that the base build shows count on shake and the button-A read-any-time behaviour is the Extend addition, so learners don't think both are core.
- cites: ["https://microbit.org/teach/lessons/step-counter/", "MID:makecode-variable-init", "MID:L1-s4"]

## slide-4 (concrete prediction anchor)
- severity: medium
- type: pedagogy
- lanes: [codex, hermes]
- problem: The lesson introduces accelerometer/sensor/variable abstraction without first eliciting a concrete learner prediction about movement and display outcome.
- fix: Open Think with a brief walk-or-shake prediction (e.g. "how does a phone/watch count steps?"), connecting the observed LED number to the input-to-output idea before defining variables.
- cites: ["MID:cognitive-load-theory", "MID:concrete-operational-learning", "MID:first-principles"]

## slide-6 (shake ≠ reliable step)
- severity: medium
- type: technical
- lanes: [codex]
- problem: Lesson implies a shake is equivalent to a step, but the MakeCode event only runs when the Shake gesture is recognised and won't reliably measure every physical step.
- fix: Describe the program as a shake-based movement counter and frame step accuracy as something to test and calibrate.
- cites: ["https://makecode.microbit.org/reference/input/on-gesture", "https://microbit.org/teach/lessons/step-counter/"]

## slide-12 (multiplier over-generalized)
- severity: medium
- type: technical
- lanes: [codex]
- problem: Multiplying the displayed count by two is offered as a general correction for undercounting, but it's only appropriate after testing shows this specific device placement consistently counts strides rather than steps.
- fix: Require learners to compare a short known walk against the device count before applying any individually justified multiplier.
- cites: ["https://microbit.org/teach/lessons/step-counter/"]
