# Student-Facing vs. Trainer-Facing Boundary

**Source Label:** Designer-Provided

## Summary
A hard content-visibility line, additional to (not a replacement for) the old vault's hidden-instruction boundary ([[32_Boundary_System]]). Trainer Guide is always internal-only. ^student-trainer-boundary

## Student-Facing Must NOT Include
Trainer scripts · trainer timing · internal trainer notes · assessment checklists · classroom management notes · private methodology · long pedagogical explanations

Student-facing content must be: visual, simple, engaging, age-appropriate, clear, branded, suitable for the delivery method.

## Trainer-Facing May Include
Session flow · timing · trainer script · trainer questions · expected answers · common mistakes · debugging notes · classroom management tips · assessment checklist · reflection questions

## Source
`Academy_Language_and_Output_Rules.md` (Student-Facing Content Rule, Trainer-Facing Content Rule)

## Why This Matters for Understanding Moon OS
This is a *new* boundary axis the old vault never had — the original deconstruction subject was never observed producing two audience-segmented output streams from one request. Moon's hidden-instruction boundary ([[32_Boundary_System]]) still applies unchanged on top of this one; the two are independent checks, not alternatives.
